  • Where. A small room with soft stuff in it. A closet full of clothes is the classic. No fan, no fridge hum, no dishwasher.
  • What. Voice Memos is fine. Hold the phone a hand's width from your mouth, a little off to the side so P and B don't pop. Same spot every time.
  • How. Warm, slow, smiling. You're talking to Baker across the kitchen table, not announcing. He hears tone long before words.
  • One clip per line. Record, wait a beat, say it, wait a beat, stop. The build tool trims the silence. Keep each under two seconds.
  • Names first. Do a few takes of each name and keep the one that sounds most like you.
  • Naming the clips. Rename each recording to the "save as" name shown on its card. Any of .m4a, .mp3, .aac or .wav works.
  • When you're done (or have a batch), put the clips in one folder and run the build command from the README. Lines you haven't recorded keep the phone's voice.
  • This page remembers what you've marked recorded on this phone. Reset is at the bottom of the List tab.
